What counts?

Actual clicks: trusted primary mouse clicks on eligible targets while on. Predictions: those clicks with an eligible guess frozen before pointer-down. Correct: exact target matches.

Hit rate = correct / actual clicks. Coverage = predictions / actual clicks. No guess counts as a miss. Keyboard activations are separate and excluded. A changing guess is not another trial.
